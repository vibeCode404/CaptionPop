Privacy Policy — CaptionPop

Last updated: October 14, 2026

CaptionPop is built on a simple principle: your videos are yours. This policy explains what data we collect — which is, in short, nothing.

Data Collection

CaptionPop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • Your videos never leave your device. Transcription, caption styling, and video export all happen locally in your browser or on your phone.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, crash reporters, or any third-party tracking.
  • No network requests are made with your video or transcript data. The only network activity is downloading the open-source AI model and libraries on first use (from public CDNs), which contains no personal information.

Permissions

The Android app requests only the permissions needed to function:

  • Storage / media access — to let you pick a video and save your captioned export. Your files are read only when you choose them.
  • Internet — used only on first launch to download the AI model and required libraries. After that, the app works fully offline.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties — because we don't have any data to share.

Children

CaptionPop does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
